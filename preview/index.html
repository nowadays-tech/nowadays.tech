readout.fm

readout is a personal AI-generated podcast. You follow the topics you are curious about and save links you want covered.

On the days and at the hour you choose, it researches the live web, keeps only the stories that moved, and writes them into a short radio script. Two hosts read it aloud.

The episode lands in a private podcast feed, so it plays in the app you already use. Every story keeps its sources.
